Privacy policy

Wyrmbound: Starfall · updated November 27, 2026

The short version

Wyrmbound: Starfall does not collect, sell or share any personal information. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

What stays on your device

Your progress (your hunter, Echoes, gear, settings and the name you choose) is saved only on your device. It is never sent to us or to anyone else. Deleting the app deletes it.

Save codes

If you tap Copy save code, the game turns your save into a code and puts it on your clipboard so you can move it to another device. Where you paste that code is up to you. The game does not send it anywhere.

Notifications

If you turn on the offline reminder, the game asks your iPhone for permission and schedules a notification on your device. Nothing is sent to a server. You can turn it off in the Lodge or in iPhone Settings.

Children

The game does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children under 13, because it does not collect information at all.

The web version

The browser version of the game is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may keep standard server logs, such as IP addresses, under its own privacy statement. The game itself still stores your progress only in your browser.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date. For questions, use the support link on the game's App Store page.
